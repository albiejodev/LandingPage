Most transport businesses manage â‚¹10 crore+ operations with the same tools they used 15 years ago. Here's what that actually costs you.

No Visibility Until You Call

You find out a truck is delayed when the client complains — not before. By then, the damage is done. You need to see your entire fleet moving, live, without picking up the phone.

Dispatch Is a Daily Crisis

Assigning trips over WhatsApp. Double-bookings. Drivers claiming they never got the message. Every morning starts with 45 minutes of back-and-forth that should take 5.

Invoices Raised Days After Delivery

Your billing team manually calculates rates from notes and WhatsApp screenshots. Charges get missed. Disputes take weeks. Cash flow suffers — not because you didn't do the work, but because the paperwork couldn't keep up.

Delivery Delays Damaging Client Trust

No one knows where a truck is until it's late. Clients get angry. You lose contracts. The problem isn't your drivers — it's the complete absence of a real-time system.

Driver Coordination Running on Trust

No structured way to confirm driver availability, verify documents, or track who's handling which trip. One driver issue shouldn't derail three deliveries — but it does.

Business Decisions Made Blind

You know roughly what revenue looks like. Roughly. You don't know your actual cost-per-trip, best-performing routes, or which clients drive your margins. Every strategic decision is a guess.
